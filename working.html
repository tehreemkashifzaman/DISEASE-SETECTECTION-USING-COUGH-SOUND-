The Future of Health is in Your Voice

CoughDectect.Ai leverages the power of Artificial Intelligence to transform cough sounds into vital health insights. Our system analyzes the frequency, duration, and subtle acoustic patterns of your cough to detect possible diseases including COVID-19, healthy or symtomatic .

Your privacy is protected. Data is never stored without your permission, and results arrive in seconds.

Step by Step: How It Works
1
Record or Upload Cough
You simply record your cough using your phone, laptop, or upload an existing audio file. No special equipment needed!
2
Sound Upload & Processing
Audio is securely transmitted to our AI engine, where it’s pre-processed and features are extracted.
3
AI Pattern Analysis
AI compares your cough’s acoustic "signature" against vast libraries of real-world cough sounds and disease markers.
4
Instant Screening Result
A preliminary report is provided instantly—showing the likelihood of key respiratory illnesses and useful next steps.
From Cough to Care: The entire process, from recording to instant analysis, is powered by machine learning and real medical data. We combine technical accuracy with ease for everyone.
