Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need special equipment?
No, any standard smartphone, tablet, or laptop microphone works well. Just record in a quiet environment.
Is my data safe and private?
Yes, your cough sound is deleted after analysis unless you give explicit permission to contribute it for research. All analysis is encrypted and compliant with privacy standards.
Does it give a medical diagnosis?
No, this is an early screening tool only. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional for official diagnoses and treatment decisions.
How accurate are the results?
Clinical studies show 70-77% sensitivity for most acute and chronic respiratory conditions. The system continues to improve by learning from anonymized samples (if you opt in for research).
Can I use the results for my doctor?
Yes. Your results are private and you may share them with your healthcare provider, who can use the information as an aid in early detection.
Can anyone use the service?
Anyone with internet and a device microphone can use CoughDetect.AI, including in low-resource areas. The service is designed for global accessibility.
How quickly do I get results?
Results are typically delivered in 5 seconds or less after you submit your cough sound!
