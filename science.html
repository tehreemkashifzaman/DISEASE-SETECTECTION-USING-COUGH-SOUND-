CoughDetect.AI
AI-Powered Cough Analysis
CoughDetect.AI uses advanced AI and machine learning, trained on thousands of cough recordings from a variety of conditions and ages. The software breaks down each cough into distinct sound features (pitch, tone, duration, patterns), then compares them to a large, validated clinical dataset. This enables:
  • Detection of subtle cough differences for a range of diseases
  • Results delivered in seconds using on-device or cloud-based AI
Data & Training
The underlying AI was developed using over cough nearly 16000 samples sourced from research hospitals, global open datasets, and clinical trials worldwide.

Medical Explanation & Use
The AI identifies sound signatures found in real clinical disease (such as the wheeze of covid, or the dry cough of COVID-19), offering screening for:
  • COVID-19 and its symptoms
  • Distinction between covid and healthy coughs
  • Instant advice and actionable insights, especially for populations with limited access to care
Remember: This is a screening tool, not a medical diagnosis. Use results to inform, not replace, your visit to a healthcare professional.
